Stood is the gate between a staged payment and proof on the ground. A payer abroad signs what “done” means. PayPal holds the money. An inspector stands on the plot. Rules decide. The money moves, or it doesn’t, and you’re told why.

The problem

Sent blind. Or held in silence.

01

Ama sends £40,000 home to build.

The WhatsApp photos stop in month four. Excuses start. Fourteen months later the house is half-built, and there’s no record of what was paid against what.

02

Or the money just… stops.

Payments into the corridor get held for weeks with no reason given. A balance that never arrives. Both failures are the same: money moves without a reason attached.

$0–0B a year in remittances to Africa. The rails that move money exist. Nothing checks what the money bought.

How it works

Four steps. One gate.

  1. 1

    Sign once

    The payer agrees what “done” means for each stage: this plot, these photos, this amount. Signed with PayPal.

  2. 2

    Hold

    When a stage is ready, PayPal authorises the tranche. It’s held, not paid, and nobody can touch it.

  3. 3

    Stand there

    An inspector captures fresh photos on the pin, with a one-time code written on paper in the first shot.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Rules check the plot, reused photos, the code and the stage. Capture, void, or a human looks.

One API before money leaves.
