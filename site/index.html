The problem

Paid on “almost done.” Or not paid at all.

01 · the buyer

Adaeze pays $4,000 for an app she never sees finished.

Milestones get invoiced on screenshots and “nearly there.” The tests pass because they test nothing. Months of chasing later, there’s a repo she can’t run, and no record of what was paid against what.

02 · the builder

Or the builder ships, and the money just… stops.

A client who goes quiet. A payment held with no reason given. Now put agents on both sides of that deal, at machine speed. Both failures are the same: money moves without a reason attached.

$0B+ paid through Upwork alone in a single quarter (Q3 2025). The rails that move money exist. Nothing checks what the money bought. Source: Upwork Q3 2025 results

How it works

Four steps. One gate.

  1. 1

    Sign once

    Your agent turns the idea into milestones and acceptance tests. You approve them once, with PayPal. The tests are locked.

  2. 2

    Hold

    When a milestone starts, PayPal authorises the payment. It’s held, not paid, and nobody can touch it.

  3. 3

    Build

    A developer, or a builder agent like Yard, pushes the work to a repo on your GitHub.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Stood runs the tests you signed on the new commit. Capture, void, or a human looks. The last payment waits for you to use the product.

Stood

The work is proven

The tests you signed, on a new commit, in your repo. Rules decide. No model can pay.

PayPal

Money moves

Hold, capture or cancel. Builders are paid through their operator’s account. Every step leaves a record.

Why this isn’t a pyramid

Agents can invoice each other forever.

Green tests nobody runs. Money moving, nothing bought. Stood refuses that loop: every chain of agent payments ends in a signal no agent in the chain controls. A human tapping release. Real users. A third service that paid for the result.

Yard builds. Stood pays.

Agents subcontract agents.

Yard can pay a test-writing agent $40 through the same gate. The buyer’s agent pays Yard. The client pays last, once she’s used it. That’s an economy, not a single trade.

Built different

Rules move money. Models only report.

Fail closed

Missing or unsure evidence means wait. Never an accidental release. A hold that expires pays nothing.

The AI can’t pay

The agent that reads your code runs with no PayPal keys. A tricked agent still can’t move money.

Never silent

Every state is one plain sentence. “The signed tests were changed. Nothing was paid.”

For platforms

One API before money leaves.

Your app owns users, matching and local payouts. Stood owns the allowance, the decision and the PayPal file. Builder agents like Yard plug in the same way as EyeOnSite, the first site-visit platform.
