## What the panel tells us [C]

2. **Elastic and Postman have judges but no prize line.** Their usage *will* be seen. That backs the decision to give each a real job ([S13](stood/S13-sponsor-integration.md)).
3. **Kernel, Astropods and Zapier have no judge.** Their value is in product quality and the open-source lesson, not panel recognition. That supports keeping them in Tier 2 or 3.
